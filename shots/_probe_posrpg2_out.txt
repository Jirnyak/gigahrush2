   1|// gigahrush2 entry point.
   2|//
   3|// Brings up an SDL3 window + Vulkan, builds the level stack, seeds an alife
   4|// population and embodies one of its records as the player (who owns the
   5|// CameraTag + Controller — the "player is just an embodied record" model,
   6|// [npcs.md]), and runs a fixed-timestep sim loop against a variable-rate render
   7|// loop with an ImGui HUD.
   8|//
   9|// The default world is the floor-MODULE stack: several distinct 128^3 floors,
  10|// each themed by its FloorKind (floor_gen.h / floors.md) and wired through a
  11|// FloorRegistry. Only the ACTIVE floor is kept live — its World is generated and
  12|// its crowd embodied on entry, and folded back into the cold pool on exit
  13|// (streaming, floor_stream.h / master_prompt #9). `[` and `]` ride down/up a
  14|// floor. `gigahrush2 maze` selects the single-world labyrinth test bed instead.
  15|//
  16|// Controls are DATA, not code: every key lives in the KeybindTable
  17|// ([keybind.h]) as a row mapping a scancode to a console command
  18|// ([console.h]), rebindable in the pause menu (Esc) and persisted to
  19|// gigahrush2.keys. Defaults: WASD move, mouse look (hold right mouse / Tab),
  20|// Space jump, F fly, Q door, E interact, [ / ] floor travel, ~ console.
